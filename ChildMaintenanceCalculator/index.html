Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is child maintenance a fixed percentage of a parent's salary?

No single percentage from this calculator should be treated as a legal rule. A real maintenance decision can consider the child's reasonable needs and both parents' financial circumstances, among other relevant factors. This tool only demonstrates a simple income-proportional starting point.

What if one parent earns much more than the other?

This illustration allocates a larger share of the listed expenses to the parent with the larger share of combined take-home income. That is only a simplified example, not a determination of what a court or the parents should agree to.

Do school fees or medical aid count?

They can be relevant child-related expenses. Enter reasonable monthly amounts, and remember to avoid double-counting a bill if it is already included in another category or in the contribution figures.

Can this calculator change an existing court order?

No. The result does not change a court order or an existing agreement. If circumstances have changed, seek appropriate guidance about the process for reviewing the arrangement.

Is my information saved or sent anywhere?

This page performs the calculation in your browser. It does not send your entries to a server or store them for later use. Avoid using a shared device if you do not want others to see information you have entered.
