A quick guide

How to use this planner

  1. Enter the allowance amount available for your chosen budgeting period.
  2. Add any other money you want to include in the plan.
  3. Enter your expected spending beside each category.
  4. Add more categories if you need them.
  5. Review your remaining balance and copy the summary for reference.

Use the period that makes sense for your payment schedule. If you are budgeting for a month, make sure the income and all expenses cover that same month.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does this use official NSFAS allowance amounts?

No. You enter your own amount. Allowances and funding arrangements can vary by student, institution, accommodation and funding rules.

Can I use it for a weekly budget?

Yes. Enter the money available for the week and only include expenses for that same week.

Are my financial details uploaded?

No. The calculator performs its calculations in your browser and does not send your figures to a server. The appearance preference is saved on your device when browser storage is available.
