Quick questions

How do I find the price per GB?

Check your mobile network's app, website, USSD menu or the retailer selling the bundle. If a bundle costs R30 for 2 GB, its price is R15 per GB.

Why is my result an estimate?

Data prices and promotions change between networks, bundle sizes and purchase channels. This calculator uses the price you enter and does not fetch live network offers.

How many MB are in a GB?

This calculator uses 1 GB = 1,024 MB for its conversion. Some network providers advertise data using a different rounding convention.

Does this calculator buy data for me?

No. It helps you work out an estimate before you buy. Purchase your bundle directly from your chosen network or retailer.
