What affects borehole costs?

Drilling depth and ground conditions

Deeper holes generally require more drilling, casing and pumping capacity. Rock formations, site access and unexpected ground conditions can change the final quote.

Pump and water storage

Pump choice depends on the borehole's depth, yield and intended use. Tank size, stand design, pipe runs and electrical requirements also affect installation costs.

Water testing and treatment

A borehole that produces water does not automatically produce water suitable for drinking. Test water quality and choose treatment based on the results and intended use.

Plan for uncertainty

Get itemised quotations from experienced local contractors. Ask what is included, what happens if the hole is dry or low-yielding, and whether testing, casing, pump installation and electrical work are covered.

Borehole costs in South Africa: FAQs

How much does a borehole cost in South Africa?

There is no single reliable price for every property. Depth, geology, access, casing, pump specifications and water treatment can make costs vary substantially. Use actual local quotations rather than relying on example values.

Does drilling guarantee water?

No. A borehole may produce insufficient water or no usable water. Ask the contractor about their drilling process, yield testing and terms for unsuccessful drilling.

Can I drink borehole water?

Do not assume untreated borehole water is safe to drink. Arrange suitable laboratory testing and use treatment appropriate to the identified contaminants and intended use.

Does the estimate include permits or municipal requirements?

Not automatically. Requirements can depend on the location, water use and applicable rules. Check with the relevant municipality and the Department of Water and Sanitation before starting work.

Can I use this for a farm or business?

Yes, as a basic budgeting worksheet. Larger systems may need higher-capacity pumps, storage, distribution infrastructure, professional assessments and additional approvals that are not fully modelled here.
