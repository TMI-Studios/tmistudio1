How to use this lobola calculator

  1. Enter the number of cattle and your estimated cost per animal, if applicable to your agreement.
  2. Add any gifts, customary items, travel, celebration and other expenses you want to budget for.
  3. Enter contributions or payments already made to see the estimated balance.
  4. Copy your estimate to share or use as a starting point for a family budget discussion.

How much does lobola cost in South Africa?

There is no single price that applies to every family. Lobola discussions and agreements can vary by family, community, tradition and individual circumstances. Cattle may be part of an agreement, or families may agree on other arrangements. This calculator does not set a customary price; it simply adds the figures you choose to enter.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official lobola price list?

No. It is a budgeting tool, not an official tariff or a statement of what any family should request or pay.

Can I calculate lobola without cattle?

Yes. Set the number of cattle to zero and enter any other costs relevant to your own agreement.

Does the calculator save my information?

Your figures are calculated in your browser and are not sent to a server by this page. The light or dark appearance preference is saved on your device when browser storage is available.
