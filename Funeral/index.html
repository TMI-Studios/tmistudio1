Understand your options

Funeral cover or family savings?

Funeral cover

Funeral insurance may pay an agreed benefit when a covered person dies and a claim meets the policy conditions. The benefit is not the same as the total premiums paid. Cover may have waiting periods, exclusions and other conditions, and premiums can change depending on the policy.

Family funeral savings or a stokvel

Savings can give a family flexibility, but the amount available depends on contributions, withdrawals, group rules and how the funds are managed. A stokvel balance is not automatically equivalent to an insured benefit.

Can a family use both?

Yes, a family may choose to combine insurance with separate savings. Use the calculator to understand the potential funding gap, then confirm which benefits and funds would actually be available when needed.
