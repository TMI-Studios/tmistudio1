Price with purpose

1. Know your costs Count the supplies you use, your time and a fair share of running costs.
2. Check your market Research comparable offers, quality and what your customers value. A calculator cannot tell you what every customer will pay.
3. Review regularly Supplier prices, travel costs and demand can change. Revisit your pricing when your costs change.
Disclaimer and calculation notes

This calculator is an independent planning tool from TMI Studios. It does not provide financial, accounting, tax or legal advice, and it does not guarantee sales, profit or a particular income.

Results depend on the accuracy and completeness of the figures entered. The tool does not automatically include tax, VAT, refunds, unsold stock, loan repayments, unexpected costs or every possible business expense. Add relevant costs to your estimates.

The suggested price is a mathematical estimate based on the costs and margin entered. It is not a market valuation or a recommendation that customers will accept that price.

Profit shown is before any unentered costs and taxes. The hourly labour amount is included as a cost of the job, so the displayed profit is what remains after that labour allowance.
