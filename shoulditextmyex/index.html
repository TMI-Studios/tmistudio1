YOUR REALITY CHECK

Take a breath first.

A low pressure message idea

Only use this if contact is welcome and appropriate. You do not owe anyone a message, either.

Before you hit send...

Missing someone is a feeling, not an instruction. You can miss a person and still choose what is healthiest for you.

What if I just want closure?

Closure does not always come from another conversation. Ask yourself what you hope they will say, and whether you can move forward if they never say it. If they have asked for no contact, do not use closure as a reason to cross that boundary.

What if I was the one who ended it?

You can still think carefully before reaching out. Consider why the relationship ended, what has genuinely changed, and whether contacting them would be fair to both of you. Give them room to decline or not respond.

What if I sent a text and they did not reply?

Give them space. Avoid sending repeated follow ups to force an answer. Silence can feel painful, but you cannot make someone participate in a conversation.

Does this quiz know what my ex is thinking?

No. This is a reflection tool, not a prediction. It cannot know what another person feels or guarantee how they will respond. It helps you pause and consider your own reasons, boundaries, and readiness.

A final reminder 💜

You do not have to make a decision tonight. Write the message in your notes, talk it through with someone you trust, or do absolutely nothing for a while. You are allowed to protect your peace.
