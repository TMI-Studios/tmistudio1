Good stokvels start with clear rules.

Before members contribute money, agree on payment dates, member responsibilities, record-keeping, payout arrangements and what happens when someone misses a payment. Keep a written record of decisions made by the group.

The calculator provides estimates based on the numbers entered. Actual payouts depend on your group's rules. The constitution template is not yet available for purchase or download, and this website does not provide legal advice.
