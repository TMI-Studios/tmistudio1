How is the stokvel pot calculated?

Multiply the number of members by each member's monthly contribution to estimate the amount collected each month. Multiply that amount by the number of months to estimate total contributions.

The average savings per member is the total group contributions divided by the number of members. This is not necessarily the payout for a rotating stokvel, where payout amounts and schedules depend on the group's agreed rules.
