Stokvel basics

Types of Stokvels

Stokvels bring people together to save, contribute and work towards shared financial goals. The right structure depends on what your group wants to achieve and the rules you agree on.

🛒

Grocery Stokvel

Members contribute towards groceries or bulk food purchases. The group can agree on when to shop, how to divide purchases and how to manage the money.

Shared shopping
💰

Rotating Money Stokvel

Members contribute a set amount, and the pooled contribution is allocated to one member at a time according to an agreed schedule. Members should clearly document payout dates and obligations.

Rotating payouts
⚰️

Burial Society

Members contribute towards agreed funeral-related support for members or their families. Eligibility, covered expenses and payment rules should be written down clearly.

Funeral support
🏦

Savings Stokvel

Members save together towards a shared goal or a planned future payout. Agree in advance on contribution amounts, withdrawal dates and how the funds will be managed.

Saving towards a goal
Before your group starts: Agree on contributions, responsibilities, missed payments, record-keeping and payout rules. Keep a written record of the decisions everyone accepts.
