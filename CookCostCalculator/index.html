How much does chicken curry and rice cost for 4?
Illustrative ingredient estimate
Example total: R126 for 4 servings, or R31.50 per serving. Prices are estimates and should be checked against current local supermarket prices.
  • Chicken, 1 kgR65
  • Rice, 500 g usedR18
  • OnionR8
  • TomatoesR15
  • Curry powderR7
  • Cooking oil allocationR10
  • Stock cubeR3

Cooking idea: fry the onion with a little oil, add the spices and chicken, then add tomatoes and water. Simmer until the chicken is cooked through. Prepare the rice separately.

  1. Compare the price per kilogram of chicken rather than looking only at the pack price.
  2. Freeze unused chicken promptly in suitable portions.
  3. Measure rice according to the number of people eating.
  4. Use your own receipts to update the calculator's example costs.

Monthly illustration: at R126 per batch, cooking this meal eight times would cost about R1,008 using the example prices. Your actual total will depend on your ingredients and portions.

How to reduce your grocery bill

  • Compare unit prices: a bigger pack is not always cheaper per kilogram or litre.
  • Plan around what you own: check your fridge, freezer and pantry before shopping.
  • Reuse ingredients: ingredients bought for one meal may work in another meal later in the week.
  • Check specials carefully: compare the price, pack size, expiry date and amount you will actually use.
  • Portion before storing: freeze suitable meat and leftovers in amounts you can use later.
  • Track real spending: use receipts to replace the sample prices on this page with your own current prices.

Cook Cost Calculator FAQs

How do I calculate the cost per plate?

Add the cost of the ingredients used in a recipe, then divide that total by the number of servings. If you are using only part of a pack, calculate the cost of the quantity used rather than charging the whole pack to that meal.

Are these current South African supermarket prices?

No. The listed amounts are illustrative estimates supplied for example calculations. They are not live price data or a verified comparison between supermarkets. Check current shelf prices, catalogues and receipts in your area.

Can I calculate a different number of servings?

Yes. Choose the dish, enter a serving count and calculate again. The calculator scales the example batch cost proportionally. Actual costs may not scale perfectly because ingredients are sold in fixed pack sizes and recipes may need different quantities.

Does the calculator include electricity, gas or cooking oil?

Some example recipes include an estimated cooking oil allocation, but the calculator does not comprehensively price cooking fuel, electricity, water or every seasoning. Add these costs separately if you want a more complete estimate.

Is cooking at home always cheaper than buying takeaway?

Not necessarily. It depends on the ingredients, portion size, number of servings, transport, energy costs and the takeaway being compared. Compare equivalent meals and include the costs that matter to your household.

Does this calculator save my shopping or financial data?

This calculator runs in your browser and does not intentionally send the values you enter to a server or store them for a future visit. The calculations are not a substitute for checking actual ingredient prices.
