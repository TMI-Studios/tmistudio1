How to make your savings goal happen

  1. Pick a specific target. Decide what you need and how much it will cost.
  2. Start with what you have. Include only savings genuinely set aside for this goal.
  3. Choose a realistic monthly amount. Make sure your plan leaves room for essential expenses and unexpected costs.
  4. Automate it if possible. A regular transfer after payday can help you stay consistent.
  5. Review the plan. Update it when your income, target or actual savings rate changes.

You can also use the calculator to compare different deadlines and monthly contribution amounts.

How is the calculation worked out?

The calculator assumes a constant annual interest rate converted to a monthly rate, with monthly deposits made at the end of each month.

  • Monthly amount mode: estimates the regular deposit needed to reach your target within your chosen timeframe.
  • Time-to-goal mode: adds monthly deposits and estimated interest until the target is reached.
  • At 0% interest: the calculation uses your starting savings and contributions without interest growth.

These are simplified projections. Real accounts may calculate interest daily, apply fees or taxes, change rates, or impose withdrawal conditions.

Frequently asked questions

What if I have already reached my target?

The calculator will show that no additional monthly contribution is needed. You can set a higher target if you want to keep saving.

What if I cannot afford the suggested monthly amount?

Try a longer timeframe or a smaller target. It is better to make a sustainable plan than to commit to an amount that puts essential expenses at risk.

What interest rate should I use?

Use the rate you reasonably expect from your actual savings account or product. If you are unsure, try a lower rate as well. Do not assume that an illustrative rate is guaranteed.

Does this include tax, fees and inflation?

No. The estimate does not automatically account for taxes, account fees, changing rates or inflation. The amount you need in the future may be higher if prices rise.

Is my information sent to TMI Studios?

This calculator performs its calculations locally in your browser. It does not send the amounts you enter to a calculation server or store them in an account. Your browser, hosting provider or extensions may still process ordinary website data according to their own policies.
