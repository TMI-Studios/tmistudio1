Price with intention

Charge for the business you want.

Your hourly rate should help cover the life you want to live and the real costs of running your work. It is a starting point, not a limit on your value.

How is my hourly rate calculated?

The calculator adds your annual personal income goal to your annual business expenses, then accounts for your chosen reserve. It divides that amount by your expected billable hours per year to estimate the hourly revenue you need.

What if I cannot bill 40 hours every week?

That is normal. Client work is only part of running a business. Enter a realistic number of billable hours, not all the hours you spend working. Fewer billable hours usually means you need a higher hourly rate.

Does this calculate South African income tax?

No. The reserve is a percentage you choose for planning. Actual tax depends on your circumstances, taxable income, deductions and applicable SARS rules. Speak to a qualified tax practitioner for a proper estimate.

Should I charge every client this exact rate?

Use it as a baseline. You may price fixed projects, packages, retainers or urgent work differently. Compare your rate with your market, experience, the value you deliver and the time each project is likely to take.

Can I use this for a side hustle?

Yes. Enter the extra income you want to earn, your side-hustle expenses and the hours you can realistically bill. The result estimates the rate needed to reach that goal.

Important: This calculator provides general financial planning estimates only. It does not account for VAT, specific tax rules, unpaid invoices, platform fees or every possible business cost. Consider these separately when setting your prices.
