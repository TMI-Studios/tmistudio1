Important information
This tool provides an educational estimate only. It does not represent an official result or guarantee admission to any university, college or programme. Admission Point Score calculations differ between institutions and qualifications. Some institutions exclude Life Orientation, use specific subject requirements, apply different point scales, or calculate points from selected subjects only. Marks entered here are processed in your browser and are not sent to TMI Studios by this calculator. Always verify your score and eligibility with the institution and course you intend to apply for.
