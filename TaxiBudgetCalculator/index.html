How to calculate your monthly taxi budget

  1. Enter the fare for one journey, not a full return trip.
  2. Enter how many one-way taxi trips you take on a typical commuting day.
  3. Add your commuting days per week and your preferred monthly multiplier.
  4. Include other transport expenses and optionally your take-home income.

The calculator estimates a typical month by multiplying your weekly commute by the number of weeks you select, then adding any other monthly costs.

How much should you budget for taxis each month?

Your monthly transport budget depends on your route, fare, how many taxis you take and how often you travel. For a regular commute, estimating costs from your actual daily fares is usually more useful than relying on a generic national fare. Remember to allow for occasional extra trips where appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator use live taxi fares?

No. Enter your own current fares. The calculator does not fetch live route prices.

Why use 4.33 weeks per month?

A year has 52 weeks and 12 months, which averages about 4.33 weeks per month. You can change this value to fit your own budgeting method.

Does the calculator save my income or travel details?

No personal budgeting details are uploaded by this page. Your calculations happen locally in your browser. The theme preference is saved on your device when browser storage is available.
