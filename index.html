A little about us

Small team energy.
Big internet curiosity.

TMI Studios is an independent product studio exploring the intersection of everyday problems, internet culture and useful digital experiences.

We build small products that help people figure things out, make something, have a laugh or get through the day with one less thing to worry about.

Every product starts with a question: can we make this easier, more interesting or more fun?

Built with curiosity We follow interesting questions and turn ideas into real tools.
Useful by design Our products should give people a reason to come back.
Always experimenting We learn, improve and keep making the internet more interesting.
