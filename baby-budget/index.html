A gentle reminder

You don't have to buy everything at once.

  • Start with essentials and add nice-to-haves only if your budget allows.
  • Ask family and friends about hand-me-downs or shared purchases.
  • Check what your medical aid or other benefits cover, if applicable.
  • Compare prices and include delivery fees when shopping online.
  • Review your estimates regularly as your plans become clearer.
This is a budgeting tool, not a medical checklist. Ask your healthcare professional about your baby's individual needs.
