A little clarity

How the split works

How are shared groceries divided?

Each item marked for everyone is split equally among the people in the group. For example, a R90 shared item split between three people adds R30 to each person's share.

Can I assign something to just one person?

Yes. When adding an item, choose one person under "Who is this item for?" That person is assigned the full cost of that item.

What does "who pays whom" mean?

The calculator compares each person's assigned share with the amount they paid for the items. It then suggests transfers to help balance the bill. These are estimates based on the information entered.

Can I split a grocery item between only two people in a larger group?

This version splits shared items equally among everyone, or assigns an item to one person. To split an item between a custom subset of people, add separate entries for the relevant portions.

Is my shopping information saved?

No. This tool calculates in your browser and does not send your shopping list to a server or save it for later. Copy your summary if you want to keep a record.
