Know your numbers

Electricity maths, minus the headache.

A little tariff awareness can help you plan your electricity budget and understand your household's usage.

Quick tip: If you are unsure of your tariff, check your latest municipal bill or ask your electricity provider. Do not assume everyone in South Africa pays the same rate.
How do I calculate electricity units from rand?

Subtract any charges that come out of your purchase amount, then divide the remaining amount by your tariff per kWh. For example, R200 at R3.50 per kWh, with no extra charges, buys about 57.1 kWh.

What is a kWh?

A kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy. A 1,000-watt appliance running for one hour uses 1 kWh. Actual appliance usage varies with its power rating and how long it operates.

Why do I get fewer units than expected?

Your purchase may include service charges, arrears or other deductions. Your electricity price may also vary by consumption block, municipality, tariff category or applicable taxes.

How can I estimate how long my electricity will last?

Add the units you are buying to your current meter balance, then divide the total by your estimated daily usage. This gives a rough number of days, assuming your usage stays similar.

Does this calculator know my municipality's tariff?

No. Enter your own tariff and any applicable charges. Tariffs can change and differ between providers, areas and customer categories, so confirm your current rate with your electricity supplier.
