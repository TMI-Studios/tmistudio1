How the calculator works

The tool converts your agreed pay to an estimated ordinary hourly rate, then applies illustrative multipliers to additional hours. Actual legal pay depends on the employment agreement, applicable law and the circumstances of the work.

Domestic worker pay in South Africa: FAQs

Is there a minimum wage for domestic workers?

Domestic workers are covered by South Africa's national minimum wage framework. The applicable hourly minimum can change, so check the latest official rate before using this estimate to set wages.

How is overtime usually calculated?

Overtime rules depend on the applicable employment legislation and agreement. Overtime generally requires agreement and is subject to limits and prescribed compensation rules. This tool uses an illustrative 1.5-times multiplier and does not confirm legal compliance.

How should Sunday work be paid?

The applicable Sunday rate depends on whether Sunday is ordinarily a working day for the employee and the rules that apply to the employment arrangement. Confirm the correct rate rather than relying on this estimate alone.

Does this calculate UIF, tax or leave?

No. The calculator does not calculate UIF contributions, income tax, annual leave, sick leave, notice pay or severance pay. Those require separate checks.

Can I use it for a live-in domestic worker?

You can use it as a basic pay estimate, but it does not value accommodation or meals, or decide whether any deduction or benefit is permitted. Check the employment agreement and current law.

Official information

For current wage rates and employment rules, consult the South African Department of Employment and Labour and the official national minimum wage notices. If you are unsure about a calculation, seek qualified labour or payroll advice.
