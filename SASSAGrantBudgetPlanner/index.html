How to use the SASSA grant budget planner

  1. Enter your actual monthly grant amount and any other income.
  2. Estimate your household's monthly essential expenses.
  3. Check whether your planned expenses fit within the money available.
  4. Adjust your figures until you have a budget that reflects your real situation.
  5. Copy your budget summary to keep it handy or share it with someone you trust.

Which grant amount should I enter?

Grant amounts and eligibility depend on the specific grant and may change over time. Enter the amount you actually receive rather than relying on a sample amount in this planner. The example figures are only there to demonstrate how the calculator works.

Frequently asked questions

Does this planner check my SASSA payments?

No. It is a budgeting calculator and does not connect to SASSA or check your payment status.

What if my expenses are more than my income?

The planner will show a shortfall. You can review the expense categories to see which costs are flexible, but prioritise your essential needs and obligations.

Does this page store my financial details?

This page calculates figures locally in your browser and does not send them to a server. Your theme preference is saved on your device when browser storage is available.
