Planning notes

A few ways to protect your budget.

  • Ask vendors for written quotes and confirm what is included.
  • Check deposits, cancellation rules and payment deadlines before paying.
  • Agree on your guest list early. Guest numbers can change catering and venue costs.
  • Decide which expenses matter most to you as a couple.
  • Keep contingency money aside for costs you haven't anticipated.
  • Update your estimates when you receive real quotes.
