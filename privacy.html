Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TMI Studios approaches information when you visit our websites and use our digital tools. Our projects may change over time, so this policy will be updated when our information practices change.

1. Who we are

TMI Studios is an independent digital product studio creating websites, browser-based tools, experiments and other digital products.

For privacy enquiries, you can contact us at tmistudios01@gmail.com.

2. Information we may process

The information involved depends on which website or tool you use. It may include:

  • Technical information: our hosting provider may process information such as IP addresses, browser and device details, timestamps and requested pages for security, operation and troubleshooting.
  • Information you email us: if you contact us, we receive the information and contact details you choose to include in your message.
  • Tool inputs: some browser-based tools use the information you enter to calculate or display results. The way that information is handled depends on the specific tool.
  • Third-party services: if a particular product includes an external service, that provider may process information according to its own privacy policy.

3. How our tools handle your inputs

Some of our tools are designed to work directly in your browser without an account. Where a tool performs calculations locally, the values you enter are processed in your browser to produce the result.

Do not assume every TMI Studios product works the same way. Products may have different features and technical arrangements. Check the information provided by the individual product before entering personal or confidential information.

Unless a tool specifically explains otherwise, avoid entering passwords, banking credentials, identity numbers or other sensitive personal information.

4. Why information may be processed

Information may be processed where necessary to:

  • Operate, maintain and secure our websites and tools.
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests.
  • Investigate errors, misuse or technical problems.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Improve our products where relevant information is available and its use is appropriate.

5. Hosting and third-party providers

Our current public website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visitors in accordance with its own policies.

You can read the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If we introduce payment processors, analytics, advertising, embedded content or other third-party services, those providers may receive information when you interact with the relevant features. Their own privacy terms may also apply.

6. Cookies and advertising

The use of cookies and similar technologies can vary between products and integrations. Our static pages may work without setting their own cookies, but hosting infrastructure and third-party services can have separate technical practices.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are introduced, those services may use cookies, IP addresses or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise advertising, depending on the service and applicable settings.

We will update this policy to explain relevant advertising and tracking technologies before relying on them. Visitors can also manage cookies through their browser settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information as a business practice. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate our websites or deliver a feature you choose to use. It may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security of our services and users.

The information shared depends on the service involved and the circumstances of your interaction with it.

8. How long information is kept

We aim to keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was received, including responding to enquiries, maintaining records and meeting legal obligations. Hosting providers and other third parties may retain technical records under their own retention practices.

9. Your privacy choices and requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may be entitled to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to raise an objection or complaint about how it is handled.

To make a privacy request, email tmistudios01@gmail.com. Please describe your request and identify the relevant website or product. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We may need to verify your request before acting.

If you are in South Africa, the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) may apply to the processing of your personal information. Your rights and the appropriate process depend on the circumstances.

10. Children's privacy

Our general studio website is not intentionally designed to request personal information from children. If a particular product is intended for children or collects information from them, additional safeguards and disclosures may be necessary.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a contact channel, please email us so we can review the situation.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to our operations to avoid unnecessary collection and to protect information we handle. However, no website, email service or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. External websites

Our websites may link to other websites and services. We do not control their content, security or privacy practices. Review the relevant provider's privacy policy before sharing information with it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as our websites, products and services develop. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date where appropriate.
