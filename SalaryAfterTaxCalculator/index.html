How this salary calculator works

South African employees commonly see PAYE and UIF deductions on their payslips. PAYE is income tax withheld by an employer, while UIF is a contribution towards the Unemployment Insurance Fund, subject to the applicable rules and earnings ceiling.

This calculator multiplies your monthly salary by 12, estimates annual income tax using the published 2026/27 tax brackets, subtracts the applicable age-based rebates, and divides the estimated annual tax by 12. It then estimates employee UIF at 1% of monthly salary, subject to the monthly ceiling used below.

The method is intentionally simplified. Actual PAYE calculations can depend on taxable remuneration, tax directives, retirement fund contributions, medical scheme tax credits, bonuses and other payroll details. This tool does not replace an employer's payroll calculation or a SARS assessment.

How much UIF will come off my salary?

The employee UIF contribution is generally 1% of remuneration, subject to the applicable earnings ceiling. For this estimate, the calculator uses a monthly ceiling of R17,712, giving a maximum employee contribution of R177.12 per month.

UIF eligibility and contribution rules can depend on the employment arrangement and applicable law. Check the official SARS UIF guidance if you need to confirm your circumstances.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result exactly what will appear on my payslip?

No. It is an estimate based on a constant monthly salary and simplified tax assumptions. Your employer's payroll calculation may include additional remuneration, deductions, credits or adjustments.

Does the calculator include medical aid and pension deductions?

No. It does not calculate retirement-fund deductions, medical scheme tax credits, bonuses, travel allowances or other payroll adjustments. These can affect your actual PAYE or take-home pay.

Why does my age matter?

South African individual taxpayers may qualify for additional rebates from age 65 and age 75. Select the applicable age group to include the relevant standard rebates in this estimate.

Is this an official SARS tool?

No. TMI Studios is an independent developer. This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by SARS, and it does not provide an official tax assessment or professional financial advice.

Which tax year does this calculator use?

It uses the published 2026/27 individual income tax rates for the tax year running from 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027.
