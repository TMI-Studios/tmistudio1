Traffic fines in South Africa: FAQs

Can I get a discount on a traffic fine?

Possibly, depending on the authority, notice type, applicable process and payment conditions. A discount is not automatic. Check the instructions on your notice or contact the issuing authority.

Does paying early guarantee a discount?

No. Do not assume early payment guarantees a reduction. Check whether your notice offers a discount, the qualifying dates and the exact amount payable.

Can this calculator check my fine online?

No. This tool does not connect to municipal, provincial or national traffic fine systems. It does not verify your fine, payment status or eligibility.

What if I believe the fine is incorrect?

Follow the dispute or representation instructions provided by the issuing authority. Pay attention to the deadlines and retain copies of your notice and any correspondence.

Is the result an official payment amount?

No. The result is only an arithmetic estimate based on the numbers you enter. The official amount shown by the responsible authority takes precedence.
