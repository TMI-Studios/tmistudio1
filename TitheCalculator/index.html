A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate 10% of my salary?

Multiply the income amount you have chosen by 0.10. For example, 10% of R15,000 is R1,500. This calculator updates the amount when you change your income or percentage.

Should I calculate tithe from gross or take-home pay?

Practices differ between people and faith communities. This tool lets you choose the income amount you want to use. It does not decide which approach is religiously correct.

Can I calculate a tithe for weekly or fortnightly pay?

Yes. Choose weekly, fortnightly or monthly, then enter the income you receive at that frequency. The annual estimate assumes 52 weekly payments, 26 fortnightly payments or 12 monthly payments per year.

What is the difference between a tithe and an offering?

Terminology and practices vary across faith traditions and congregations. This calculator treats the tithe percentage and any additional offering as separate planning entries so you can customise them.

Does this calculator save my salary?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. The page does not send your entered income to a server or save it for later use.
